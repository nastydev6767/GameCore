Features

Everything your game needs to run at its best

GameCore does in one click what used to take 20 minutes of manual tweaking.

⚡

Smart Process Killer

Closes browsers, updaters, and sync apps. Never touches your stream software, system files, or Discord.

🧠

RAM Optimizer

3-step standby list clear frees 1–3 GB that Windows keeps occupied for no reason.

🔥

Extreme Mode

Close everything except the game. Max CPU, max fans, zero distractions. Your PC is 100% dedicated to gaming.

🎬

Streamer Mode

Protects OBS, Streamlabs, Discord, and camera apps so your stream stays live while everything else is closed.

🎮

Steam Integration

Launches Steam games through the Steam protocol so all your saved launch options (like -dx11) are always preserved.

📊

Live Monitor

Watch CPU usage, RAM, temperature, and FPS in real time while your game runs. Open Task Manager to verify the numbers.

🔄

Full Restore

When your game closes, GameCore restores your power plan, restarts paused services, and relaunches safe background apps.

🕹️

Any Launcher

Works with Steam, Epic, GOG, and any custom launcher. Browse to the .exe and add it manually in one click.

How it works

One click. Better gaming.

GameCore handles everything before, during, and after your game.

Pick your game

GameCore auto-detects Steam, Epic, and GOG libraries. Add any other game manually with the file browser.

Click Launch

GameCore closes background apps, frees RAM, sets CPU to performance mode, then launches your game.

Play normally

GameCore monitors CPU, RAM, and temperature silently in the background. Nothing to do.

Everything restored

When you close the game, GameCore restores your PC exactly to how it was before.
