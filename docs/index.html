Features

Everything your game needs to run at its best

GameCore does in one click what used to take 20 minutes of manual work.

⚡

Smart Process Killer

Closes browsers, sync apps, and background processes. Never touches your stream, Discord, or system files.

🧠

RAM Optimizer

Three-step standby list clear frees 1–3 GB of RAM that Windows keeps occupied for no reason.

🔥

Extreme Mode

Close everything except the game. Max fans, max CPU, zero distractions. Your PC is 100% the game's.

🎬

Streamer Mode

Protects OBS, Streamlabs, Discord, and camera apps so your stream stays live while everything else is closed.

🎮

Steam Integration

Launches Steam games through the Steam protocol so all your saved launch options (like -dx11) are preserved.

📊

Live Monitor

Watch CPU, RAM, temperature, and FPS in real time while your game runs. Open Task Manager to verify.

🔄

Full Restore

When your game closes, GameCore restores your power plan, restarts paused services, and relaunches safe apps.

🕹️

Any Launcher

Works with Steam, Epic, GOG, and any custom launcher. Just browse to the .exe and add it manually.

How it works

Three steps to a better gaming session

GameCore handles everything before, during, and after your game.

Before you play

Click Launch in GameCore. It scans your hardware, kills background apps, frees RAM, and sets your CPU to performance mode.

While you play

GameCore monitors CPU, RAM, and temperature in the background. If something spikes, it adjusts automatically.

After you finish

When your game closes, GameCore restores everything — power plan, services, even Spotify and OneDrive.

Even without opening it

GameCore sits in your system tray and auto-optimizes the moment it detects a game launched anywhere.
